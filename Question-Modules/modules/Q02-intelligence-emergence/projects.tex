\item \textbf{Capability-card audit.} Turn one broad model claim into a reproducible capability card with task distribution, metric, baselines, contamination risks, and a failure taxonomy.
\item \textbf{Metric-threshold simulation.} Simulate a continuous score curve and compare how exact-match, pass@k, and thresholded metrics change the apparent onset of a capability.
\item \textbf{Generalization challenge set.} Build paired examples that preserve an underlying rule while changing surface wording; evaluate rule-based, retrieval, and model baselines.
