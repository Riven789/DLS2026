\begin{enumerate}[leftmargin=1.5em]
\item \textbf{Indicator audit (medium, 1 week).} Take the Butlin et al.\ (2023) indicator-property list and assess a specific open model architecture against each, with evidence and honest uncertainty per indicator. Deliver a scored rubric, not a verdict.
\item \textbf{Self-report steerability (small, 2--3 days).} Empirically show a single model asserting and denying consciousness under different prompts; quantify how prompt framing moves the claim, demonstrating why self-report is not evidence.
\item \textbf{$\Phi$ on toy networks (medium, 1--2 weeks).} Compute an IIT-style integrated-information proxy on small feed-forward vs.\ recurrent toy circuits; show the feed-forward/recurrent gap and discuss what it does and does not tell us (ties to Rick's machine-consciousness experiments).
\end{enumerate}
