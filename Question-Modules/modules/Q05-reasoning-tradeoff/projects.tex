\begin{enumerate}[leftmargin=1.5em]
\item \textbf{Coverage curves (small, 2--3 days).} On a verifiable benchmark (e.g.\ GSM8K), measure accuracy vs.\ $n$ for best-of-$n$ with and without a verifier; fit the diminishing-returns curve and locate the cost-optimal $n$.
\item \textbf{Overthinking study (medium, 3--5 days).} Sweep forced reasoning length on an easy and a hard task set; show the inverted-U and identify where extra length starts to hurt.
\item \textbf{Adaptive compute (larger, 1--2 weeks).} Build a difficulty estimator that allocates more samples/longer traces only to hard queries; compare total compute vs.\ fixed-budget baselines at matched accuracy.
\end{enumerate}
