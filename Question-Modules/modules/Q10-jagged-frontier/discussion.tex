\begin{enumerate}[leftmargin=1.5em]
\item Human competence is correlated across difficulty; model competence is jagged. What single fact about training best explains the difference?
\item Many ``dumb'' failures are tokenization artifacts. Does this mean they are superficial and will disappear with scale, or structural? Argue with an example.
\item A model fixes a simple error the moment you ask it to reason step by step. What does that tell you about where the capability actually lived?
\item If you cannot infer reliability on task B from brilliance on task A, how should that change the way products are built on top of LLMs?
\item Propose a task you are confident a strong model will fail despite its apparent simplicity, and justify your prediction from the three root causes.
\end{enumerate}
