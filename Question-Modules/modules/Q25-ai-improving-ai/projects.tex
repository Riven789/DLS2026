\begin{enumerate}[leftmargin=1.5em]
\item \textbf{Distillation (medium, 4--6 days).} Distill a larger model's outputs into a smaller student on a task; measure the student's gain vs.\ training on ground-truth labels alone, and where the teacher's errors propagate.
\item \textbf{Judge-bias audit (small--medium, 3--5 days).} Quantify length bias, position bias, and self-preference in an LLM-as-judge setup; propose and test mitigations (randomized order, length normalization).
\item \textbf{Collapse experiment (medium, 1 week).} Iteratively retrain a small generative model on its own outputs across generations; measure diversity/quality decay and how much fresh real data arrests it.
\end{enumerate}
