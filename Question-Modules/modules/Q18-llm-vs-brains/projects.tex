\begin{enumerate}[leftmargin=1.5em]
\item \textbf{Brain-score mini (medium, 4--6 days).} Take a pretrained vision model and a public neural-response dataset; measure how well layer activations linearly predict neural firing. Relate predictivity to layer depth.
\item \textbf{Biologically-plausible learning (medium, 1 week).} Implement feedback-alignment (random backward weights) vs.\ backprop on a small task; quantify the accuracy gap and discuss what it implies for the brain-plausibility debate.
\item \textbf{Energy accounting (small, 2--3 days).} Estimate joules-per-inference for a small transformer and compare to the $\sim$20\,W human brain budget for a loosely matched task; write up the orders-of-magnitude gap and its sources.
\end{enumerate}
