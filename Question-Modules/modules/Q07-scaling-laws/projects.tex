\begin{enumerate}[leftmargin=1.5em]
\item \textbf{Fit your own scaling law (medium, 3--5 days).} Train a small model family at several sizes on a fixed corpus; fit loss vs.\ parameters to a power law and test whether it extrapolates to a held-out larger size.
\item \textbf{Chinchilla check (medium, 1 week).} At a fixed compute budget, sweep the $N$ vs.\ $D$ split and empirically locate the compute-optimal point; compare to the Chinchilla prediction.
\item \textbf{Data-wall estimate (small, 2--3 days).} Using public estimates of high-quality text stock and a chosen scaling recipe, compute when a target model size runs out of compute-optimal tokens; write up the sensitivity to assumptions.
\end{enumerate}
