\textbf{Question.} A model correctly completes several new examples after seeing three demonstrations in its prompt. Does this alone prove that the model has learned a general rule? Give one reason why not and one test that would add evidence.\par\bigskip
\textbf{Expected answer.} No. It could exploit superficial prompt patterns, memorized fragments, or a narrow heuristic. Test on held-out rule combinations and paraphrases, compare with baselines, and inspect calibration/error patterns.\par\bigskip
\textit{Diagnoses the misconception that a few correct demonstrations establish robust generalization.}
