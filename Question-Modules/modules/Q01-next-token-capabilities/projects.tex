\item \textbf{Controlled generalization benchmark.} Create a synthetic grammar with held-out rule combinations; compare a frequency baseline, a small sequence model, and a retrieval baseline. Report accuracy, calibration, and failures.
\item \textbf{Prompt-as-data experiment.} Hold a task fixed while varying the number, order, and quality of demonstrations. Pre-register the metric and test whether gains transfer to paraphrased prompts.
\item \textbf{Metric sensitivity audit.} Replot a scaling result under continuous and thresholded metrics. Explain which conclusions survive and which depend on the metric.
\end{enumerate}\end{document}
