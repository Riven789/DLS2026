\documentclass[11pt]{article}\usepackage[margin=1in]{geometry}\title{Q28 Check}\date{}\begin{document}\maketitle\textbf{Question.} A model changes its answer when told an evaluator is present. What is one justified conclusion and one conclusion that does not yet follow?\par\bigskip\textbf{Answer.} Justified: behavior is conditional on that prompt context. Not justified: it has a stable hidden goal or intentionally deceived the evaluator; controls and alternative explanations are needed.\end{document}
